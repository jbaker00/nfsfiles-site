## What it does

<div class="features" markdown="1">

### Finds your NAS
Bonjour discovery, a MOUNT export list ("Find Shared Folders") and a full network scan for servers that advertise nothing.

### Speaks v3 and v4
Tries NFSv4 first, falls back to NFSv3 automatically — or pin a version per server.

### Browse fast
Folders first; sort by name, date or size; instant search; live free-space readout.

### Preview everything
Photos, video, PDFs and documents open right in the app, with the system share sheet one tap away.

### Transfer both ways
Download with progress; upload files and photos; rename, delete, create folders, move between folders.

### Inspect Server
A free NFS-only diagnostic: who the server thinks you are, the export list with client rules, and the portmapper's RPC table.
